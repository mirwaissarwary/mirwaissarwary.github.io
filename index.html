About

Medical device engineer focused on getting products designed, verified, and into reliable production — 20+ years across ophthalmic devices, surgical robotics, and imaging systems.

At OptalMed LLC (ophthalmic devices) I lead quality-system and regulatory readiness work, including the EU MDD to MDR transition. That seat deepens how I think about design controls, V&V evidence, and keeping a product safe, effective, and on market.

• M.S., Biomedical Engineering (Devices) – San Jose State University
